## Important Rule

When an error is solved, do not only write "fixed".

Record:
1. what failed,
2. why it failed,
3. what changed,
4. how it was verified,
5. how to prevent recurrence.

If the error reveals a reusable engineering lesson, also update `PROJECT_MEMORY.md`.
